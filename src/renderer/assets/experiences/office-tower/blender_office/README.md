# Office Building interior

Open-plan office for OnePad with daytime city / park views through curtain glass.

## Layout notes
- Clear carpet island ~r=11.5 (desk screens at ~r=7 stay clear)
- Columns at r≈15.2 (outside perimeter desks)
- Ceiling ~6.2m — no acoustic baffles over the desk ring (they clipped screens)
- Exterior: Sketchfab modular city with roads, parks, and trees (north of glass)

## Paths
- Source script: `blender/office/1/build_office_interior.py`
- Blend: `blender/office/1/office_interior.blend`
- Runtime GLB: `office_interior.glb`
